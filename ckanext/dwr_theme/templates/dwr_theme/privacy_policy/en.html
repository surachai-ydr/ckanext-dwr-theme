{{ g.site_title }} (“the website”) respects your privacy and protects your personal data in accordance with Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act B.E. 2562 (2019). This policy explains how we collect, use and disclose it.

1. Information we collect

  • Usage data such as your IP address, browser type, pages visited and time of visit, recorded automatically.
  • Account data such as your username, full name and email address when you register or log in.
  • Cookies needed for the website to work, such as keeping you logged in and remembering your language and text size.

2. How we use it

  • To run the website and let you search, preview and download datasets.
  • To manage user accounts and publishing permissions.
  • To keep the service secure and to analyse usage statistics so we can improve it.

3. Disclosure

We do not sell your personal data or disclose it to third parties unless you have consented or the law requires it.

4. Retention

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law, after which it is deleted or destroyed.

5. Your rights

You may request access to, a copy of, correction, deletion or portability of your personal data, restrict or object to its processing, and withdraw your consent at any time. You may also complain to the Personal Data Protection Committee.

6. Security
